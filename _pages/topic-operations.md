Cloud operations is about making platforms self-sustaining: PaaS-managed compute, automated remediation, and infrastructure that reduces toil rather than creating it. These posts cover operational patterns for Azure environments at enterprise scale.

- [Understanding dependencies in Azure]({% post_url 2021-06-20-Understanding-dependencies-in-Azure %}) — Resource dependency modeling for reliable deployments and structured troubleshooting

## Focus areas

- **PaaS operations:** Azure Automation, Update Management, Monitor, and Advisor integration
- **Container platforms:** AKS operational patterns, private networking, and container registry management
- **Observability:** Log Analytics workspaces, diagnostic settings, and alerting baselines
- **Reliability:** Availability zones, redundancy patterns, and fault-domain awareness
- **Reducing toil:** Automation runbooks, self-healing patterns, and configuration drift detection
